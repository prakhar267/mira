# Mira brand guide

Mira is the current product and repository name.

- **Product:** Mira
- **Descriptor:** An adults-only AI companion
- **Repository:** [prakhar267/mira](https://github.com/prakhar267/mira)
- **Website:** [Mira](https://luma-companion.prakhargupta267.workers.dev/)
- **Release:** Free public beta; provider quotas and launch limitations apply

Use Mira in product copy, repository descriptions, launch materials and documentation. Historical service identifiers are implementation details, not alternative product names. Naming does not establish trademark clearance or a legal operating entity; those remain launch-review items.

## Personality and language

Warm, direct, curious and conversational. Respond to the user's actual meaning and follow their English, Hindi or Hinglish language choice. Avoid exaggerated accents, stereotyped phrases, repetitive clarification and generic reassurance.

Keep system messages practical: what happened, what remains available and what the user can do next. Do not describe an unavailable provider as if it were still speaking or listening.

## Visual direction

The active interface uses warm coral/peach accents with plum-toned companion surfaces and lighter marketing sections. Cormorant Garamond is the display typeface and Manrope is the interface typeface. The implemented reference is [the web stylesheet](../apps/web/app/globals.css), not an archived prototype screenshot.

Use current Mira screenshots with synthetic content for launch materials. Preserve readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior and clear call states. Label generated characters and speech honestly; an animated avatar must not be represented as a real person on a live camera.

## Product promises

- Chat, voice and animated avatar conversation.
- English, Hindi and Hinglish support, with explicit acknowledgement that recognition and replies can be wrong.
- Memory users can inspect, correct and delete.
- Clear AI identity and adult eligibility.
- Straightforward export, deletion, support and failure controls.

Do not claim perfect memory, human identity, mind-reading, medical care, end-to-end encryption, unlimited free usage or guaranteed availability without verified evidence. Do not use guilt, exclusivity, jealousy or dependency pressure.

Verify current product behavior and [launch gates](LAUNCH-OPERATIONS.md) before changing public claims. Keep third-party asset licenses and attribution intact. The repository's brand name is not a substitute for approved Terms, Privacy Policy, provider agreements or independent security review.
