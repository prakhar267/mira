# Safety

Companion is adults-only, identifies itself as AI, and is not therapy, emergency support, or a substitute for human relationships.

The current deterministic safety router provides testable handling for self-harm, violence, dependency, medical, legal, financial, and sexual-minor risk categories. It is a prototype guardrail, not a certified safety system.

Production launch requires multilingual evaluation, provider moderation, incident ownership, red-team testing, crisis-policy review, appeal/reporting flows, and monitoring that does not expose private conversations. Responses must never encourage exclusivity, isolation, jealousy, guilt, or continued engagement at the expense of real-world wellbeing.
